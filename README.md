## What Is COSMIC-BRAIN?

COSMIC-BRAIN is the **media, lore, and production archive** of the NOMADZ stack. It stores and organizes everything creative -- all the assets, stories, sounds, visuals, and COSMIC-KEY creations that power the NOMADZ universe.

---

## What Lives Here

- **Media Assets** -- all NOMADZ art, visuals, audio, video renders
- **Lore Books** -- NOMADZ worldbuilding documents, character bibles, story arcs
- **Productions** -- completed and in-progress productions (game, film, audio)
- **COSMIC-KEY Creations** -- templates, boilerplates, and bootstraps for new NOMADZ tools
- **Signalverse Canon** -- the official NOMADZ universe lore
